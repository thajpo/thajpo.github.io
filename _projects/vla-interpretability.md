## Overview
Independent research project focused on understanding how vision-language-action models represent tasks, bind perception to action, and fail under distribution shift.

## Current Direction
- Build small, inspectable evaluation environments.
- Compare activations and behavior across task variants.
- Look for interpretable structure in action selection and latent representations.
